HOW IT WORKS

Three jobs. One decision contract.

Adversarial AI attacks the claim, an authority adapter checks the facts, and the kernel bounds what the verdict can do — PASS, PASS_WITH_DEBT, or BLOCK, always with its scope on the receipt.

01

Attack

Adversarial AI attacks the claim and surfaces the failures existing checks missed.

02

Frame

Bind the claim's scope, assumptions, and the authority that could prove it.

03

Check

Turn findings into executable evidence checks — an adapter can run them against the real system.

04

Verdict

The kernel signs PASS, PASS_WITH_DEBT, or BLOCK, bounded to what was actually proven.

05

Gate

No adapter, no action: without an authority adapter the verdict stays epistemic.
