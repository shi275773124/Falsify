HOW THE GATE WORKS

Claim. Frame. Verify. Cutline. Receipt.

One inspectable decision contract: PASS, PASS_WITH_DEBT, or BLOCK — with an authority path you can re-read.

01

Claim

Surface the AI or bot statement as a claim, not a fact.

02

Frame

Capture scope, assumptions, and the authority that can prove it.

03

Verify

Read the system that actually holds the truth.

04

Cutline

No evidence, no pass — separate Must Fix from Known Debt.

05

Receipt

Leave finding, authority, evidence, and a reproduce command.

Core receipt and reproducible evidence for a bounded claim.
