HOW THE GATE WORKS

Frame. Attack. Recompute. Cut. Receipt.

The public core keeps one inspectable decision contract: PASS, PASS_WITH_DEBT, or BLOCK.

01

Frame

Name the claim, authority, owner, and decision boundary.

02

Attack

Seek the cheapest counterproof before trusting the summary.

03

Recompute

Read the state, calculation, command, or raw source that matters.

04

Cutline

Separate Must Fix, Known Debt, and Delete.

05

Receipt

Preserve verdict, evidence path, and claim ceiling.

Core receipt and reproducible evidence for a bounded claim.
